Thu Chi

Thu Chi is a free Android app for tracking personal and family income and expenses, debts, budgets and reminders. Data is stored on your phone first (offline-first). Vietnamese interface.

Features

  • Fast entry by typing, voice or receipt photo
  • Wallets, bank accounts, credit cards and debt book
  • Reports, budgets and reminders
  • Optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive

Google sign-in

Thu Chi uses Google Sign-In only to let you back up and restore your own data in your own Google Drive. We do not run any server and cannot see your data.
