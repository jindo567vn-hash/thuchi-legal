Vi Gia Dinh (Ví gia đình)

Family income & expense tracker for Android

About the app

Vi Gia Dinh (Ví gia đình) is a free Android app that helps families track income and expenses, wallets and bank accounts, credit-card statements, overdrafts, debts, budgets and reminders. Your data is stored on your phone first (offline-first), and the interface is in Vietnamese.

Key features

  • Fast entry by typing, voice or receipt photo
  • Wallets, bank accounts, credit cards with statement cycles, and overdraft accounts
  • Debt book, budgets, reports and smart reminders
  • Optional encrypted backup and family sharing through your own Google Drive

How Google sign-in is used

The app uses Google Sign-In only so that you can back up, restore and share your own data through your own Google Drive. The developer does not run any server and cannot see your data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
