Privacy Policy

Vi Gia Dinh (Ví gia đình) for Android

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Vi Gia Dinh (Ví gia đình) Android app ("the app") handles information. The app is operated by an individual developer (contact below).

1. Data stored on your device

Transactions, accounts, debts, budgets and settings are stored locally on your phone. The developer does not operate any server and has no access to this data.

2. Google account and Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests your email address (to identify the account) and the following Google Drive scopes:

  • drive.appdata – to store an encrypted backup in a hidden app-specific folder of your own Drive.
  • drive – to read and write the single backup folder that the family owner creates and shares with the Gmail accounts of family members, so that members can sync the same household data. The app only reads and writes the files it created in that folder and does not browse, read or modify any other file in your Drive.

Backup content is encrypted with a password you set before it is uploaded. Google user data is processed only on your device and in your own Drive. It is never sent to the developer or to third parties.

3. Limited Use disclosure

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup, restore and family-sharing features you request, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

4. Optional AI features

If you turn on the AI assistant and enter your own API key (for example Gemini or Groq), the text you type, your voice recording or the receipt image you choose is sent to that provider to be interpreted. This happens only when you use these features and is governed by that provider's policy. You can turn the feature off at any time.

5. Permissions

Microphone (voice entry), camera and photos (receipt scan and background image), notifications and exact alarms (reminders), and biometrics (optional app lock). Each is used only for the feature described.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share your data and do not use it for advertising. We do not collect analytics.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to remove local data. To remove cloud backups, delete the app's backup files from your Google Drive, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: jindo567vn@gmail.com.
