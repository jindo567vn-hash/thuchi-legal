Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how the Thu Chi Android app ("the app") handles information.

1. Data stored on your device

Your transactions, accounts, debts, budgets and settings are stored locally on your phone. The developer does not operate a server and has no access to this data.

2. Google account and Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests your email address and the Google Drive scopes drive.file and drive.appdata. These are used only to create, read and update the backup files created by the app in your own Google Drive. Backups are encrypted with a password you set, before upload. The app does not read any other file in your Drive. Your Google data is never sent to the developer or to third parties.

3. Optional AI features

If you turn on the AI assistant and provide your own API key (for example Gemini or Groq), the text you type, your voice recording or the receipt image you choose is sent to that provider to be interpreted. This only happens when you use these features, and is governed by that provider's own policy. You can turn the feature off at any time.

4. Permissions

Microphone (voice entry), camera and photos (receipt scan, background image), notifications (reminders). Each is used only for the feature described.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising. We do not collect analytics.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to remove local data. To remove cloud backups, delete the app's backup files from your Google Drive, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Changes and contact
